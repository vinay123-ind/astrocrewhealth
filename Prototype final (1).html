Astronaut Health Monitor

Prototype with randomly generated data. Suggestions are general safety guidance for a flight surgeon to review. The health score is a simple deduction (25 points per critical problem, 10 per other problem). Radiation, fluid and hydration alert levels are demo values; real limits are set by the space agency and flight surgeon. The software never prescribes medicines or doses, and it is not a validated medical device. In a real emergency, follow crew medical procedures.
